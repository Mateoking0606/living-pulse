WHO WE ARE

About Us

We are a group of university students who created Living Pulse, a volunteer campaign focused on promoting physical activity among university students.

Our mini-project aims to encourage healthier habits through short and flexible physical activities that can be incorporated into students' daily routines.

Living Pulse seeks to create awareness, encourage participation and show that lack of time does not have to be a barrier to movement.

♥

Our Purpose

Encourage university students to move, participate and build healthier daily habits.

02

OUR DIRECTION

Mission & Vision

MISSION

Move with purpose.

Our mission is to promote physical activity among university students through short, simple, and flexible routines that can easily be incorporated into academic life. Living Pulse aims to eliminate lack of time as a barrier to staying active, encourage healthy habits, and build a supportive community where students motivate and support each other.

VISION

Grow the movement.

Our vision is to build a more active and healthier university community where physical activity becomes part of students’ daily lives and small moments of movement develop into sustainable long-term habits. We want Living Pulse to inspire students to stay active, take care of their well-being, and encourage others to do the same.

03

UNDERSTANDING THE CHALLENGE

The Problem

University students are increasingly exposed to sedentary lifestyles.

Long hours attending classes, studying, completing assignments and using computers and mobile devices can reduce the time available for physical activity.

01

Long hours sitting

Students can spend extended periods sitting during classes, study sessions and academic activities.

02

Academic responsibilities

Classes, assignments and studying can leave students with limited free time.

03

Digital habits

Frequent use of computers and mobile devices can contribute to less physical movement.

04

Possible effects

Prolonged sedentary behavior can be associated with poor physical fitness, stress and health risks.

OUR ACTIVITY

Living Pulse Activity

Two-Minute Physical Routine

Living Pulse is a short physical activity designed to encourage university students to move and stay active.

The activity consists of a two-minute routine with simple movements such as marching in place, squats, jumping jacks, stretching and breathing exercises.

The activity will be carried out with university students from other classes or academic programs.
